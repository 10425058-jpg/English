# English Quest — Verified Educational Sources & Licensing Notes

> Curated external resource metadata in `content/resources/learningResources.ts` | Last URL Audit: 2026-11-27

## Core Principle

English Quest hosts original in-house curriculum modules authored specifically for Vietnamese-speaking and global English learners, while linking outward safely (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`) to **36 verified, reputable public educational portals and dictionaries**. No external copyrighted lessons are scraped or redistributed inline.

## Verified Provider Categories (36 Entries)

1. **General English & Integrated Skills**
   - **British Council LearnEnglish** (`learnenglish.britishcouncil.org`) — Main portal plus direct skill/reference sections (Grammar A1–A2 & B1–B2, Business English, Listening, General Reading, Writing, Magazine). Free access with institutional copyright; links open upstream cleanly.
   - **BBC Learning English** (`www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish`) — Hub plus flagship audio series (*6 Minute English*, *The English We Speak*, *News Review*). Free public broadcast study material.
   - **VOA Learning English** (`learningenglish.voanews.com`) — Clear-paced US broadcast portal, *Everyday Grammar*, and *As It Is*. US public domain informational broadcasts.
2. **Dictionaries & Phonetics**
   - **Cambridge Dictionary** (`dictionary.cambridge.org`) — Dual UK/US IPA and Learner Definitions.
   - **Oxford Learner's Dictionaries** (`www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com`) — Oxford 3000/5000 & CEFR headword indicators.
   - **Merriam-Webster Dictionary** (`www.merriam-webster.com`) — American usage & etymology notes.
   - **YouGlish** (`youglish.com`) & **Forvo** (`forvo.com`) — Authentic connected-speech corpus queries and speaker pronunciation clips.
3. **Academic Writing, Grammar & Corpus Wordlists**
   - **Purdue OWL** (`owl.purdue.edu`) — General Writing, Academic Writing & ESL Mechanics Guides (fair educational reference linking).
   - **Khan Academy Grammar** (`www.khanacademy.org`) — Free Creative Commons / open-access foundation grammar mastery lessons.
   - **Cambridge English — Activities & Write & Improve** (`www.cambridgeenglish.org`, `writeandimprove.com`) — Free learner task bank and instant AI writing band indicator.
   - **Victoria University of Wellington — Academic Word List (AWL)** & **New General Service List (NGSL) Project** (`www.wgtn.ac.nz`, `www.newgeneralservicelist.com`) — Research-backed frequency corpora (CC-BY / academic research access).
4. **IELTS Preparation**
   - **IELTS Official (IELTS.org)** (`www.ielts.org`) — Official sample test questions & Academic Reading guides.
   - **IDP IELTS Prepare** (`ielts.idp.com`) & **British Council TakeIELTS** (`takeielts.britishcouncil.org`) — Free candidate orientation and sample tasks.
5. **Authentic Extended Reading & Open Commons**
   - **TED-Ed** (`ed.ted.com`), **Simple English Wikipedia** (`simple.wikipedia.org`, CC BY-SA), **Project Gutenberg** (`www.gutenberg.org`, public domain classics), and **OER Commons** (`www.oercommons.org`, Open Educational Resources).
